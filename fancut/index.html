02Decision #1 | Where to play, how to winStrategy

Bet on fan co-creation, and win with what rivals can’t copy.

Fans create in one app and listen in another.

Douyin feeds Soda Music: 156M users in 4 years.*1 TikTok closed its own music app in 2024.*2 Amazon Music sits between TikTok and Spotify.

04Decision #3 | Finding the hook lineProduct

Show the hook as a lyric line, not a waveform.

Fixes “Edit” · Videos are made to be shared, so the hook must be right, and fans must see why.

Fans need to see which part AI Edit picked: no music terms, show where, every song. Each draft got two of three, so the final keeps the best of each.

—What I Learned

My second Amazon Music hackathon: one sharp bet beats a full system.

[比赛结果 / 评委反馈]

In 2025 I did the same hackathon and built a journal, recaps and stickers. Comparing the two taught me most.

1. Start from a gap, not features. Last time we ended up with another yearly recap; this time we mapped the market first.

2. Go deep on one flow. Six features in four weeks became one flow we could test end to end.

3. Back each choice with numbers. Surveys and data made the pitch easier to trust.
